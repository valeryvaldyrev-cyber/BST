About me

Valery Valdyrev

I am a personal massage therapist in Tallinn. I have trained in Chiromassage and professional massage techniques and work with three main approaches: SMART, Chiromassage and Aromatic.

I adjust the pressure and character of each session individually. My practice includes long-standing regular clients as well as people visiting for the first time.
