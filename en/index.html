Services & prices

Three different massage experiences

Choose a more intensive approach, smooth flowing work or quiet downtime with an aromatic element.

SMART

A more intensive massage format combining work along muscular and fascial chains with a variety of techniques. A session may include deep-tissue techniques, pressure work, trigger-point work, stretching, rolling, pulsing, as well as elements of joint articulation and gentle mobilisation.

A good choice for people who prefer more noticeable, firm and varied bodywork. The intensity is adjusted individually.

60 min — 40 €
90 min — 50 €

Chiromassage

A fluid and varied massage without a rigid sequence. A session combines strokes, compression techniques, a wide range of kneading movements and other manual techniques. The depth and character of the work can change depending on the area and the client’s preferences.

Suitable for people who enjoy a flowing and comfortable massage but do not want it to feel superficial.

60 min — 40 €
90 min — 50 €

Aromatic

A calm and flowing massage using an aromatic blend. The movements are slow, the hands remain in contact with the body for much of the session, and the overall experience is centred on comfort, warmth and relaxation.

Suitable for those who prefer a gentler massage, pleasant aromas and quiet time to unwind.

60 min — 40 €
90 min — 50 €

About me

Valery Valdyrev

I am Valery Valdyrev, a massage therapist in Tallinn. In 2023, I graduated from Eesti Massaaži- ja Teraapiakool and obtained the professional qualification of Massage Therapist, Level 5.

After completing my core training, I continued studying approaches that I now use in my practice. At Magistra school in St Petersburg, I completed the Basic Course in Body Chiromassage and a course in Miotensive Chiromassage of the Body. I also completed training in Trigger Point Therapy.

I continue developing my skills in SMART therapy and facial techniques. In my work, I combine different approaches and adapt the intensity and character of each massage individually — from stronger SMART work to flowing chiromassage and more relaxing sessions.
